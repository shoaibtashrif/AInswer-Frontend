How it works

Choose your mode, add your business knowledge, forward calls, then receive instant summaries.

Step 1

Connect your number

Forward unanswered calls to your AInswer forwarding number.

Step 2

AI answers & qualifies

Captures name, number, reason, urgency plus industry-specific details.

Step 3

Instant summaries

Sends SMS (shortSummary) and email (summary). Stores everything in your portal.

Advanced section: quoting + bookings

Structured quoting

Only quote from approved rules/FAQs. Never guess.

  • Fixed price or range
  • Conditions/exclusions
  • Fallback to callback

Appointment bookings

When enabled, checks availability and creates bookings via calendar integration.

  • Availability lookup
  • Slot confirmation
  • Confirmation message
